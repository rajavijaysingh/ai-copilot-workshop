What's a Prompt?

Your words in → AI's work out

🎯 WHO — Who will read this?

📐 WHAT — What type? (list, story, message, poem...)

📏 HOW MUCH — How long or short?

🎭 TONE — Funny? Serious? Friendly?

⚡ EXTRA RULE — One special rule to make it interesting

Systems Check — Ethics

The machine is never guilty. The pilot always is.

Why Ethics?

Power without rules = danger

🛡️ Protect — Be careful what you feed AI. Once it's in, you can't take it back.

📣 Credit — If AI helped, say so. Don't pass off its work as yours.

🧠 Think First — Just because you CAN doesn't mean you SHOULD.

🔍 Verify — Check the work. Don't trust blindly.

AI Ethics Court

3 cases. 3 defendants. You're the justice system.

1️⃣ Facilitator reads the case

2️⃣ Squads huddle (1 min) — pick 2 different speakers

3️⃣ Opening — Prosecution 45s → Defense 45s

4️⃣ Closing — Prosecution 30s → Defense 30s

5️⃣ Jury votes: GUILTY / NOT GUILTY + scores each side 1–5

Different speaker for opening vs closing. Everyone participates.
