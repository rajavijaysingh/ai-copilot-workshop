SECTION-BY-SECTION TIPS:

PRE-FLIGHT:

  • No captain — equal opportunity. Any member can speak/write for the group
  • Boarding Pass: "When I think about my future job, I worry AI would: ___" — fold, pocket, don't share
  • If students already seated (cold-open): grab attention with "PILOTS—READY" signal first, then do squad formation

G1 KNOWLEDGE (MythBusters):

  • 6 rounds. Show statement → 5-sec countdown → squads hold up 1 (TRUE) or 0 (MYTH) + bet fingers
  • Answers: MYTH · TRUE · MYTH · MYTH · TRUE · MYTH
  • Confidence bet (1-3 fingers) is for DRAMA only — scoring = correct cards × 3, cap 20
  • After each reveal: 15-sec "reality line" — keep it fast, don't lecture
  • "So what" (60 sec): You JUDGED · Both sides matter · Foundation for everything else

G2 COMMUNICATION (Prompt Battle):

  • 5 prompt ingredients always visible on screen: WHO · WHAT · HOW MUCH · TONE · EXTRA RULE
  • Show anatomy example + AI response first (Tanglish WhatsApp message)
  • Round 1 (easy): "Get AI to convince a teenager to drink more water" — 3 min writing
  • Round 2 (hard): "Career advice without asking questions" — 3 min writing
  • Live fire: random pull 1 slip → type exactly → room scores 1-5 → show trainer's example + AI response
  • BACKUP: Both trainer's example outputs saved as screenshots. If AI tool crashes → show screenshot
  • "So what" (60 sec): Harder problem = bigger gap · THIS is the co-pilot skill

G3 ETHICS (AI Court):

  • Show ALL 3 cases overview first — squads prep simultaneously (2 min huddle)
  • Format per case: FIR read aloud → Huddle 1 min → Opening (Pros 45s → Def 45s) → Closing (Pros 30s → Def 30s) → Jury votes + scores
  • 2 DIFFERENT speakers per squad (opener ≠ closer) — enforces participation
  • Jury: holds 1 (GUILTY) or 0 (NOT GUILTY) + scores each side's performance 1-5
  • Rules revealed: Case 1 → PROTECT · Case 2 → CREDIT · Case 3 → THINK FIRST
  • Final reveal: PROTECT · CREDIT · THINK FIRST · VERIFY (from G1)
  • Starter arguments on screen if squads freeze — they can build on top
  • "So what" (60 sec): AI did nothing wrong. The guilty party was always the human

G4 NAVIGATION (AI Jobs):

  • 12 rounds, same bet mechanic as G1: REAL(1) or MADE UP(0) + confidence 1-3
  • TWIST: ALL 12 are REAL. Students don't know this. Those who bet MADE UP lose points
  • Jobs span: Medical, Law, Agriculture, Finance, Music, Film, Literature, Engineering
  • Big reveal: "Every single one was real. None existed 5 years ago."
  • Then → Squad warm-up (2 min, loud, AI+Me combos) → Flight Log samples → Solo writing (5-7 min, QUIET)
  • Drop volume for solo writing. Dawn-gradient slide. Don't talk first 2 min. Walk softly
  • Volunteer pitches: 4-5 hands, 20 sec each. Pick top 3 for bonus points (+4/+3/+2)

TAKEOFF:

  • Boarding pass payoff: "Pull out your boarding pass. One is fear. One is what you'd build. The gap is your runway."
  • Pledge: show sample answers → they write on back of Flight Log
  • Co-Pilot Cup: announce winner, cheers, celebrate

EMERGENCY CUTS (in order):

1. ✂️ Peer scoring from G2 (−5 min) · 2. ✂️ Navigation jobs to 6 rounds (−4 min) · 3. ✂️ Solo writing to 3 min (−3 min) · 4. ✂️ Fewer pitches (−2 min)

NEVER cut: Boarding pass payoff · Pledge · Cup · The big reveal (all jobs are real)

6 KEY LINES (must hit):

  1. "Here's the lie — AI is coming for your seat. Wrong. You're the pilot."
  2. "The machine was confidently wrong — and YOU caught it."
  3. "Same mission, same AI — different pilot."
  4. "In all three cases, the AI did nothing wrong. The question was the HUMAN."
  5. "Every single one of these jobs is real. None existed 5 years ago."
  6. "The kids who learn to fly WITH this thing lead what comes next. That's you."

LANGUAGE TIPS (non-native English audience):

  • Speak slowly and clearly. Short sentences
  • Repeat instructions once — same words, not paraphrased
  • Use hands/gestures to show what "hold up 1" or "hold up 0" means
  • Allow any language for Flight Log writing — Tamil, Hindi, drawings, mix — it's personal
  • Tanglish is fine in prompts and pitches — this is about thinking, not English
