PROMPT LAB — YOUR DEMO FLOW:

  1. Type "Write about exams" → read boring output
  2. Type Tanglish WhatsApp prompt → read funny output
  3. Ask "What was different?" → label their answers as 5 ingredients
  4. Show mission → they write (2 min) → you pick 3-4 → voice into AI → discuss each

5 INGREDIENTS: 🎯 WHO · 📐 WHAT · 📏 HOW MUCH · 🎭 TONE · ⚡ EXTRA RULE

Mission options:

  • "Get AI to convince a teenager to drink more water"
  • "Get AI to explain cricket to someone who's never seen it"
  • "Get AI to write a sorry message to a friend you ignored"

ETHICS — YOUR FLOW PER CASE:

  1. Show case on screen, read aloud (1 min)
  2. "Hands up — guilty? Not guilty?" (10 sec)
  3. Pick 2 guilty voices: "Why?" (30 sec each)
  4. Pick 2 not-guilty voices: "Challenge that" (30 sec each)
  5. YOU probe: "But what about..." (30 sec)
  6. Reveal the rule (15 sec)

Cases → Rules:

Case 1 (Code Leak) → PROTECT

Case 2 (Voice Clone) → CREDIT

Case 3 (Art Heist) → THINK FIRST

SOCRATIC PROBES (use when discussion stalls):

  • "But does intent matter if the damage is already done?"
  • "If an intern did this instead of AI — would that change your answer?"
  • "Who's responsible — the person or the company that has no rules?"
  • "Would you feel the same if it happened to YOUR work?"
  • "Is it tribute if nobody knows it's AI?"
  • "What's the difference between inspired-by and copied-from?"

KEY LINES (must hit):

  1. "AI is coming for your seat. Wrong. You're the pilot."
  2. "Same AI. Different pilot." (after prompt contrast)
  3. "AI did nothing wrong. The human crossed the line."
  4. "None of these jobs existed 5 years ago."
  5. "The kids who fly WITH this thing lead what's next."

LANGUAGE TIPS (non-native audience):

  • Speak slowly. Short sentences.
  • Repeat instructions same words, don't rephrase
  • Tanglish in prompts = encouraged
  • Allow silence — count to 5 before re-asking
  • "Who agrees?" easier than "What do you think?"
  • Use gestures: thumbs up/down for guilty/not guilty

IF THINGS GO WRONG:

  • AI tool crashes: Read pre-saved responses from your phone notes
  • Dead silence: "OK I'll start — who disagrees with me?"
  • Running long: Cut to 2 cases instead of 3, skip jobs rapid-fire
  • Too noisy: "Pilots —" / "— ready!" signal (practice once at start)
  • No one writes: "Give me the topic verbally — I'll type it live"
  • One person dominates: "Thank you — now someone from THIS side of the room"
