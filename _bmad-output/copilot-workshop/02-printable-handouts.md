# 🖨️ AI IS YOUR CO-PILOT — Printable Handout Pack

**Print plan for 134 students / 12 squads:**
- Per **student** (×134): 1 × Boarding Pass, 1 × Flight Log (double-sided).
- Per **squad** (×12): 1 × Voting Card sheet (cut into 3 cards), 1 × Co-Pilot Code card (with starter arguments), 1 × Prompt Bet Slip (double-sided), 1 × Squad Assignment card.
- Per **facilitator**: Cue Card + Scoreboard.

> Design note: handouts carry ALL the words. The screen carries none. Print mono-friendly (works in black & white); amber on screen = **bold/boxed** on paper.

## 📋 HANDOUT 8 — FACILITATOR RUN-SHEET *(facilitator only)*

| Time | Segment | Screen | Squads do | Score (max 20) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Pre-Flight | BOARDING / split image / 4 checks | Form squads, write Boarding Pass | — |
| 0:12 | G1 Knowledge | 7 myth cards + bet | Hold TRUE/FALSE + confidence fingers | Correct × 3, cap 20 |
| 0:25 | G2 Communication | Prompt missions + Live Fire | Write prompts; 1 random + 1 gold live; peer score | Peer avg × 4 = max 20 |
| 0:50 | G3 Ethics | FIR cases × 3 | Argue (2v2) / jury vote / write rules | Won=20, Lost=10, Jury=5 |
| 1:05 | G4 Navigation | Today/Future jobs + Flight Log | Vote cards; solo write; captain pitch | Votes×2 + pitch bonus, cap 20 |
| 1:25 | Takeoff | All checks lit → pledge → cup | Write pledge; crown squad | — |

**134 students / 12 squads (10 squads of 11 + 2 squads of 12)**

**Crowd control:** Call **"Pilots —"** / response **"— ready!"** to reset. Captains hold logistics during transitions. Cards held up, never passed forward. Materials pre-loaded in squad envelopes before doors open.

**Golden rules:** (1) Screen never shows a sentence to read. (2) Everyone plays in parallel; screen only samples. (3) 60-sec "so what" after every check. (4) Protect the quiet minutes at Flight Plan.
