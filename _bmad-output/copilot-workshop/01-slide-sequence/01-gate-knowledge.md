## SECTION 1 — INSTRUMENTS CHECK: KNOWLEDGE / AI MythBusters (Slides 5–13)

### Slide 5 — Check Card
- Big: 🔧 **INSTRUMENTS CHECK — KNOWLEDGE**
- Sub: *Can you out-think the machine?*
- Strip: all checks dim, INSTRUMENTS pulsing.

### Slide 5b — How to Bet (show once, stays in memory)
- Visual: a hand holding a TRUE/FALSE card + another hand showing 1, 2, or 3 fingers.
- Three lines, huge:
  - **HOLD UP: your answer (TRUE or FALSE)**
  - **HOLD UP: your bet (1, 2, or 3 fingers)**
  - ✅ Right → +your bet  ·  ❌ Wrong → −your bet
- Sub: *Max bet = 3. Big risk, big reward. Scared pilots bet 1.*
- *(This is the only "rules" slide — keep it up for 15 sec, then move.)*

### Slides 6–12 — Myth Cards (7 rounds, one per slide)
- Each slide: ONE statement, centered, large. A **5-second countdown ring** in the corner. Nothing else.
- **Corner legend (every myth card slide): `1 = TRUE · 0 = MYTH`**
  - 6: "AI IS ALWAYS FAIR AND UNBIASED." → ❌ MYTH
  - 7: "AI HAS ALREADY HELPED DISCOVER NEW MEDICINES." → ✅ TRUE
  - 8: "AI IS BECOMING CONSCIOUS — IT CAN THINK AND FEEL." → ❌ MYTH
  - 9: "ONCE AI IS SET UP, IT RUNS PERFECTLY BY ITSELF." → ❌ MYTH
  - 10: "AI CAN BE FOOLED BY THINGS A 5-YEAR-OLD WOULD GET RIGHT." → ✅ TRUE
  - 11: "AI WORKS JUST LIKE A HUMAN BRAIN." → ❌ MYTH
  - 12: "AI IS TRULY CREATIVE — IT INVENTS NEW IDEAS." → ❌ MYTH
- On reveal: the word **MYTH** or **TRUE** slams across in amber.
  - Answers: 6 ❌ · 7 ✅ · 8 ❌ · 9 ❌ · 10 ✅ · 11 ❌ · 12 ❌
  - Facilitator gives a 15-sec "reality" line after each (see speaker notes).
  - Wrong-as-a-room myths get a small 📎 *"Filed as Exhibit"* tag — seeds the courtroom callback.

*(Order is deliberately unpredictable — a TRUE at #7 breaks the "it's all myths" assumption early, and another TRUE at #10 hits just when they've settled back into expecting myths. No pattern to game.)*

### Slide 13 — Instruments Check Lit + "So What" (60 seconds)
- The KNOWLEDGE check ignites amber on the strip (satisfying glow/sound cue).
- One line on screen: *Instruments reading clearly. You know where AI is powerful — and where it breaks.*

**Facilitator 60-second delivery notes:**

Cover THREE beats in this order (20 sec each):

1. **You didn't just memorize — you JUDGED.** "You just did something AI can't do for itself: you evaluated what's true and what's not about AI. The machine can't judge its own limits. You can."

2. **Both sides matter.** "Some of you lost points on the TRUEs — you underestimated what AI can do. Some lost points on the MYTHs — you overestimated it. A good pilot knows BOTH: what the machine can handle, and where it needs you to take over."

3. **This is the foundation for everything else today.** "For the rest of this session, every game builds on what you now know: AI is powerful, it's flawed, and the person steering it makes all the difference. That's you. Instruments — checked."

*(Then move. Don't linger. The energy should carry forward, not deflate into a lecture.)*

**Scoring (Instruments Check — max 20 points):**
- Each correct card (regardless of bet) = 3 points. Max 7 correct = 21, capped at 20.
- The confidence bet (1-3 fingers) is for DRAMA only — it makes the game exciting but doesn't affect the score. This keeps scoring simple for the helper while keeping the betting mechanic alive for engagement.
- Helper just counts: how many rounds did each squad get right? × 3. Done.
