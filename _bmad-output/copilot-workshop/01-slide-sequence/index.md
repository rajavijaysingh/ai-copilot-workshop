# ✈️ AI IS YOUR CO-PILOT — Screen Slide Sequence

**Design law:** The screen is a jumbotron, not a worksheet. Every slide passes the **3-second rule** — one idea, readable from the back row. All reading-text lives on the handouts. Slides are spectacle, timer, scoreboard, and reveal only.

**Visual language (keep consistent all deck):**
- Background: deep night-sky navy (`#0B1437`).
- Accent / "lit" color: runway amber-gold (`#FFC23C`).
- Text: near-white (`#F5F7FF`). Single bold sans-serif (e.g. Montserrat / Poppins), ALL-CAPS for headlines.
- Recurring motif: the **Pre-Flight Check Strip** — 4 checks (INSTRUMENTS · COMMS · SYSTEMS · FLIGHT PLAN) across the bottom. Checks start dim grey; each lights amber when its game ends. This is the progress bar AND the story.
- One font weight change = one level of hierarchy. No more. White space is the co-pilot.
